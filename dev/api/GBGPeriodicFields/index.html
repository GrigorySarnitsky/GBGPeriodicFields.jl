GBGPeriodicFields

Real-valued scalar, vector, and second-order-tensor fields on three-dimensional rectangular periodic domains, carried in physical-space and real-to-complex spectral representations, on both CPU and GPU array backends, with HDF5 field persistence.

The tensors are Cartesian — components on one fixed orthonormal frame, with no curvilinear coordinates and no metric machinery — so this is an applied field toolkit rather than a general differential-geometry package.

The package is the field layer for pseudospectral fluid and turbulence solvers, and it carries a point layer that evaluates fields between grid nodes for Lagrangian-particle methods. A field is one real physical object; the physical and spectral forms are two representations of that same object.

The umbrella assembles its public surface from the submodules below:

  • Fields supplies the field and grid types, the storage layout and broadcasting, the FFT transform plans, the transforms to_physical and to_spectral between the two representations, the allocator similar_field that builds compatible scratch, and the HDF5 persistence pair write_field/read_field (available once HDF5 is loaded).
  • FieldAlgebra provides the fiberwise algebra: the vector-space operations add! and subtract!, the weighted combination combine!, the scalar actions multiply! and divide!, the contractions dot and cross, the tensor product mul!, the transpose transpose!, the norm norm, the trace and determinant tr/det, the sym/skew/dev decompositions, and map!/map applying an arbitrary fiber map φ at every base point — the escape hatch for any pointwise operation not named here.
  • DifferentialOperators provides the spectral operators gradient, divergence, curl, symmetric_gradient, laplacian, inverse_laplacian, and helmholtz_projection.
  • Convolution provides periodic convolution convolve, its lazy exponential form exponential_convolve and the kernel-composed combine! method, together with the kernel builders spectral_cutoff, spectral_band, squared_wavenumber_field, assignment_window, and translation_kernel.
  • Points supplies the point-data types, their fiberwise algebra mirroring the field surface in physical representation, the matching allocator similar_pointvalues, and the HDF5 persistence pair write_points/read_points (available once HDF5 is loaded).
  • Interpolation provides the grid-to-point transfer interpolate and its adjoint point-to-grid transfer anterpolate, over the local-spline and spectral schemes.
  • Reductions provides the L²/measure reductions integral, inner, weighted_inner, l2norm, and lpnorm; the moments mean/std/var and the order statistics maximum/minimum/extrema, which extend their Statistics and Base generics on sections; and the radial reductions radial_spectrum and radial_correlation. Each reduction also has a mapped form taking a leading fiber map, which reduces a nonlinear invariant without building it.

FiberSections is the base-agnostic foundation that the field and point layers share.
