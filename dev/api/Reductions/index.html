Reductions

GBGPeriodicFields.Reductions — Module
Reductions

The L² / measure structure on sections — the third operation family alongside the vertical (fiberwise) algebra and the horizontal (differential) operators (docs/ARCHITECTURE.md §2, decision 18). A reduction integrates a section over its base, against the measure the base carries: Grid3D's uniform quadrature (cell volume ∏Δxᵢ) and PointSet's counting measure (unit weight per point — forced, since a PointSet is grid-free). One set of formulas serves fields and point-values, dispatched on AbstractFiberSection.

Defined on physical fields and point-values — representation-free / physical-space quantities, where the base reduction is the naive one. inner/l2norm additionally act on fields in spectral representation via Parseval (equal to the physical inner, no transform); integral/mean/lpnorm/ maximum stay physical-only.

  • integral(f) — the extensive ∫ f dμ (the only quantity that scales with the base measure): (|Ω|/N)·Σf on a grid, Σf over points.
  • mean(f), std(f), var(f) — extend Statistics; intensive and fiber-preserving (mean of a vector field is a FiberVector). mean(f) = (1/count)·Σf — the arithmetic average over base elements: (1/N)·Σ on the grid (the domain average, the grid being uniform), (1/M)·Σ over points (a plain sample mean).
  • inner(f, g) — the normalized L² inner product ⟨f,g⟩ = (1/count)·Σ f·g → scalar (intensive, matching mean); l2norm(f) = √inner(f,f), lpnorm(f, p) (p = 2 default, p = Inf ⇒ sup of the fiber magnitude).
  • maximum/minimum/extrema — extend Base for scalar sections.

Physics stays consumer-side: kinetic energy ½ inner(u,u), enstrophy ½ inner(ω,ω), dissipation ν inner(ω,ω), rms √inner, intensities from std.

The Reductions module is the L² / measure structure on sections — the third operation family alongside the vertical (fiberwise) algebra of FieldAlgebra and the horizontal (differential) operators of DifferentialOperators. A reduction integrates a section over its base against the measure the base carries (Grid3D's uniform cell volume; PointSet's counting measure), and is defined on physical fields and point-values (representation-free quantities). See docs/ARCHITECTURE.md §2 and decision 18.

integral is the extensive ∫f dμ; inner/l2norm/lpnorm are the normalized L² inner product and norms. The mean/std/var (from Statistics) and maximum/minimum/extrema (from Base) are extended on sections here — reached through using Statistics / Base, not re-exported. Physics stays in the consumer (kinetic energy ½ inner(u,u), enstrophy, dissipation, rms).

Public API

GBGPeriodicFields.Reductions.inner — Function
inner(f, g)

The normalized L² inner product ⟨f, g⟩ = (1/count)·Σ f·g of two compatible sections (full fiber contraction summed over the base, divided by the base count) — a scalar, intensive (matching mean). For homogeneous turbulence this is the natural pairing: kinetic energy is ½ inner(u, u).

Defined for physical fields and point-values (the naive sum above), and for two fields in spectral representation via Parseval: inner(f̂, ĝ) equals the physical inner of the same fields — the energy / inner product without an inverse transform. lpnorm, integral, mean, maximum remain physical-only (only the p = 2 inner is representation-invariant).

source
GBGPeriodicFields.Reductions.integral — Function
integral(f)

The base integral ∫_Ω f dμ of a section — extensive (scales with the base measure): the uniform-quadrature (|Ω|/N)·Σf on a Grid3D, the counting-measure Σf over a PointSet. Fiber-preserving: a scalar for a scalar section, a FiberVector for a vector section, a FiberTensor2 for a tensor section. Physical-space only; spectral integration is future.

source
GBGPeriodicFields.Reductions.l2norm — Method
l2norm(f)

The normalized L² norm √inner(f, f) = √⟨|f|²⟩ of a section — the root-mean-square of the fiber magnitude. The p = 2 case of lpnorm. Defined for physical fields and point-values, and — via the Parseval inner — for a field in spectral representation.

source
GBGPeriodicFields.Reductions.lpnorm — Function
lpnorm(f, p = 2)

The normalized Lᵖ norm (⟨|f|ᵖ⟩)^{1/p} of a section, where |f| is the pointwise fiber magnitude (the vertical norm). p = 2 reduces to l2norm; p = Inf is the sup of the fiber magnitude ‖f‖_∞ (e.g. the maximum speed for a CFL condition). Physical-space only.

Internal API

These symbols are not part of the public interface; they are documented for contributors — the base measure (count and cell measure) and the fiber-preserving reduction helper.

GBGPeriodicFields.Reductions._fiberwise — Method
_fiberwise(section, whole_reduce, over_base_reduce)

Apply a reduction that keeps the fiber and integrates the base away, returning a fiber value: a scalar for tensor order 0, a FiberVector for 1, a FiberTensor2 for 2. whole_reduce(data) is used for the scalar case (reduce the whole array); over_base_reduce(data, base_dims) reduces only the leading base dimensions for higher orders. The (tiny) reduced array is copied to the host before the SVector/SMatrix build, so no device array is scalar-indexed (invariant 6).
