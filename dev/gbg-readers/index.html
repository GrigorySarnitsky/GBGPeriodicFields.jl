The readers of GBGPeriodicFields

Written by AI, not regularly reviewed

This page is written by AI and is not reviewed regularly by a human.

A documentation review is read by agents, and which agents they are is not a list anybody keeps: it follows from the rules. A rule no program can decide carries a tag saying what a reader must be given to judge it — nothing beyond the one page, the sibling pages, the source, the built site — and the readers below are the bundles those tags fall into. A rule that names a field of expertise goes to a reader of that field, which the package declares in its config.

withheld

A scientist in the package's own fields, new to the package, on a first read of ONE page — every other page, the design documents and the source withheld. Judges only what this page says by itself: a term of the package it uses before it defines, a sentence without a verb, a symbol it binds nowhere, a comment that will not render as prose. Standard mathematics and the field's own vocabulary need no definition; the package's do.

It is given these rules to check:

  • RULE.DOC.GRAMMAR
  • RULE.DOC.LITERATE
  • RULE.DOC.RETURNS
  • RULE.DOC.SELF_CONTAINED
  • RULE.DOC.TERMS_DEFINED
  • RULE.TEX.CODE_SPAN_JULIA
  • RULE.TEX.IMAGINARY

full

A careful reader of the whole documentation with the source beside it: every docstring, every page, the reference list, the notation page and the package's config. Judges what one place says against what the others say and against the code it documents — expanding a formula against the code rather than taking the docstring's word.

It is given these rules to check:

  • RULE.API.POSITIONAL
  • RULE.CIT.NO_INACCESSIBLE
  • RULE.CIT.STYLE
  • RULE.DOC.ADMONITION_TITLE
  • RULE.DOC.ALIASING_BULLET
  • RULE.DOC.COVERAGE_BASES
  • RULE.DOC.HOME_ORDER
  • RULE.DOC.LEADING_IDENTITY
  • RULE.DOC.MODULE_MAP
  • RULE.DOC.ONE_HOME
  • RULE.DOC.ONE_NOTATION
  • RULE.DOC.STEER
  • RULE.DOC.TWO_TIER
  • RULE.DOC.VARIANT_PAIR
  • RULE.DOC.VERIFIED
  • RULE.TEX.GREEK_AB

rendered

A reader of the BUILT site, page by page, as a visitor meets it. Judges what the build made of the source: what renders wide, what renders as prose that was meant as code or as code that was meant as prose, what Literate and Markdown made of a comment.

It is given these rules to check:

  • RULE.DOC.LITERATE_RENDERED
  • RULE.TEX.NO_HSCROLL

differential_geometry

A differential geometer judges whether the structure the package names — the manifold with its coordinates and origin, frame, orientation, metric and measure, the group its operations are claimed invariant under, and the identifications between objects, such as vector with covector by the metric — is the structure its statements use, and whether each statement keeps to it. It traces each sign, argument order and measure to the structure it rests on, and asks which continuum identities the discretization keeps, where, and which it loses. It reports a structure a statement rests on that no page declares or two declare differently, an identification used where its hypothesis fails, a structure named that nothing uses, and a declared choice whose reason leaves a geometer's objection standing. Each filing names the standard, the alternative and, where a reason is stated, what it leaves unanswered. An open reader — bound to no rule beyond those it is handed by id — reads the whole documentation and reports whatever is wrong in its field that no rule names; a defect a rule does name is reported under that rule's id, as the rulebook in .gbg/policy/ states it, not as the field's. The code is context for reading the documentation, never the thing under review. The rule(s) it is handed are this reader's to judge.

It is given these rules to check:

  • RULE.DOC.COORD_FREE

spectral_numerics

A numerical analyst of Fourier pseudo-spectral methods judges whether the discretization is apt for the use the documentation declares and whether its claims about it hold. It reads the transforms, their normalization and conjugate-symmetric half, the Nyquist planes, dealiasing and the band it leaves against the scales computed, spectral differentiation, shell-binned spectral estimators as lattice quadratures, interpolation and anterpolation between grid and points, time stepping with its stability, order and stiff-term treatment, and the floating-point precision any stated number or equality depends on. A spectrum's sampling variance is the statistician's and the law it is compared with the theorist's. It reports a claimed property the scheme lacks though code matches formula; an estimator given the name of the continuous quantity it approximates with no word on where the two differ; a method that falls short of the declared use, stating that use, the requirement it implies and the measure of the shortfall; an error, convergence or cost claim without its order and regime; a claim made for one precision and true in another; and a scheme cited but varied without saying so. Each names the standard applied and the alternative. An open reader — bound to no rule beyond those it is handed by id — reads the whole documentation and reports whatever is wrong in its field that no rule names; a defect a rule does name is reported under that rule's id, as the rulebook in .gbg/policy/ states it, not as the field's. The code is context for reading the documentation, never the thing under review.

software_architecture

A software architect judges the design as the documentation states it against what the package is for and the changes it expects, in Julia's terms: a method may sit beside its operation family or beside its type, and a generic extended in several modules is not split. It reads the modules and their responsibilities, the interfaces and dependency direction between them, each invariant and the mechanism the documentation says keeps it, the quality attributes the design buys and at what cost, and the reasons recorded for each decision with the alternatives rejected. It reports a module whose parts change for different reasons or whose responsibility is not stated; a responsibility placed where a change the documentation expects would ripple across parts; a dependency against the stated layering, or a declared policy of reaching into siblings' unexported names rather than exporting them; an interface no part states or wider than the parts name; a concept two modules own or two names denote; a contract stated at two strictnesses in two places, or whose grain — entry, aggregate or call — is unfixed; a duty put on the caller with no word on what its breach does; a quality attribute claimed without its cost; a mechanism a simpler design would replace, that design and what it gives up named; an invariant with no stated mechanism, or left to care where a named enforcement would hold it, with its cost; and a decision whose recorded reason does not answer the objection stated. Each names the principle, the alternative and the recorded decision it answers, quoting what the reason leaves unanswered or that none is recorded; an alternative the recorded reason already weighs is not reported unless the reader says why the weighing is wrong. An open reader — bound to no rule beyond those it is handed by id — reads the whole documentation and reports whatever is wrong in its field that no rule names; a defect a rule does name is reported under that rule's id, as the rulebook in .gbg/policy/ states it, not as the field's. The code is context for reading the documentation, never the thing under review.

plain_sense

Asks of each sentence whether it means anything and whether it earns its place. Reports the sentence that survives deletion without loss, and the one whose meaning cannot be recovered from the words.

logician

Reads definitions and arguments as formal objects. Reports circularity, equivocation between two senses of one word, a conclusion that does not follow, and an unfalsifiable claim dressed as a specification.
